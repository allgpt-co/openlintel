## Evidence gates

- Practitioner bios: publish only accurate identity, experience and review scope supplied by the person.
- Case study: obtain permission, preserve confidentiality, record actual workflow/output and limitations. If no completed pilot exists, use a clearly labeled practitioner workshop report.
- Time saving: use the same defined task, record methods/sample/limitations; no invented percentages.
- Specifications/FF&E commercial page: a named release completes the relevant workflow reproducibly and professional review boundaries are visible.
- Procurement/PM commercial pages: show actual item/approval/order/exception workflows before claiming software capability.
- Competitor comparisons: repeated buyer need plus hands-on evaluation; verify current claims and disclose method, not a promotional best-software list.
- Completeness checker: at least three practicing studios validate useful rules. Process files locally, flag completeness only; no certification or client-document analytics.
- Localization: meaningful researched differences, reviewed imperial/metric units and terminology; no automatic country duplicates.
