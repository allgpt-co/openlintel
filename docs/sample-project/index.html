Chapter 02 / The design

Give the room a point of view.

Explore two material directions within the same space. Quiet Oak is the selected route through this illustrative project.

The selected sample direction

Pale oak, warm mineral walls, and linen. A calm material palette lets the light and the existing floor lead.

An alternative material direction

Darker joinery and olive upholstery give the same room a deeper character. An alternative study; Quiet Oak continues into the sample drawings.

01 / LIGHT

02 / MATERIAL
